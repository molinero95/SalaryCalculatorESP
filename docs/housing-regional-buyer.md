# Regional purchase deductions without municipality dependence (housing phase 4)

Phase 4 of the [housing plan](https://github.com/molinero95/SalaryCalculatorESP/pull/22) calculates the regional deductions for buying the habitual dwelling whose amount does not depend on the municipality. Code: `js/domain/housing-buyer.js`; guards and skip reasons: `js/domain/housing.js`; provenance: `js/data/housing-rules.js` and `data/fiscal-sources.json` (group `housing.regionalBuyer`); fixtures: `tests/housing-buyer.test.js`.

Fiscal year 2026. Texts checked on 10 October 2026 in the BOE consolidated versions linked below.

## What is calculated

Each deduction reduces only the **regional quota** of the annual return, never below zero, and does not change payroll withholding. The only housing rule with a payroll effect remains the state DT 18ª withholding reduction (phase 1).

## Inputs and guards

- `housingInvestment` (existing): what the person paid in the year for the dwelling: principal, interest and costs. `housingInterestPaid`: the interest part, capped at the investment.
- `housingBuyerConfirmed`: one confirmation covers habitual dwelling in the community for at least three years, the net-worth test (state rules of 31-12-2012 referred to by Andalucía, Extremadura, Murcia and La Rioja), a mortgage taken to buy the dwelling for the interest rules, and no earlier deduction for another habitual dwelling (Andalucía art. 9.5 and the state rules). Without it the buyer rules are skipped with `requirementsNotConfirmed`.
- `housingFirstDwelling` (Extremadura), `housingProtectedDwelling` (Andalucía, Extremadura; protection is a regional qualification) and `housingNewBuild` (Murcia) are explicit declarations shown only where a calculated rule uses them.
- Changing the community clears the buyer confirmation and the protected-dwelling declaration.
- Madrid art. 12 counts interest until the month before the taxpayer turns 30. The age entered is the age on 31 December, so a person who turned 30 during the year is skipped rather than prorated.
- La Rioja: buyers before 2013 keep the transitional regional deductions (DT 1ª, not modelled); RIO-2 is skipped with `transitionalRegimeApplies`.
- Rehabilitation and disability adaptation are not collected; only purchase payments are considered.
