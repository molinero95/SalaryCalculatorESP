# Housing deductions 2026: research and specification

Status: **research only**. Nothing in this document is implemented. The calculator must keep treating housing deductions as unsupported until a phase of the [implementation plan](#implementation-plan) is completed with its own tests.

Scope: personal income tax (IRPF) housing deductions for fiscal year **2026** in the 15 common-regime autonomous communities, plus the state rules they interact with. Basque territories and Navarra are out of scope (see [foral-payroll.md](foral-payroll.md)).

Research date: 10 October 2026. Rules published after that date are not covered.

### Vocabulary used in the tables

- **BI**: _base imponible_ (general + savings unless stated otherwise).
- **BL**: _base liquidable_.
- **Min. P&F**: personal and family minimum (_mínimo personal y familiar_).
- **Joint**: _tributación conjunta_. "Not stated" means the compendium does not say whether the cap or income limit changes in joint filing; treat as pending.
- **Tenant / landlord**: the tenant (_arrendatario_) deducts rent paid for their own habitual dwelling. The landlord (_arrendador_) obtains rental income (_rendimientos del capital inmobiliario_). They are separate taxpayers and separate features.

## Summary of findings

1. **New state rental deduction (L1, but legally unstable).** Real Decreto-ley 29/2026 (BOE 7 October 2026, in force 8 October 2026) adds art. 68.6 LIRPF: 10 % of rent for the habitual dwelling when BI < 33,007.20 €. A previous version (RDL 26/2026) was repealed by Congress on 2 October 2026. RDL 29/2026 still needs congressional validation, and whether rent paid before 8 October 2026 counts for 2026 is not settled. See [State rules](#state-rules).
2. **State transitional regimes still apply (L1).** Purchase before 2013 (DT 18ª) and rental contracts before 2015 (DT 15ª) keep their frozen 2012/2014 rules. DT 18ª is the only housing rule that changes **payroll withholding** (−2 points, art. 86.1 RIRPF).
3. **All 15 communities have at least one tenant rental deduction** in 2026 (L2), but eligibility varies widely: age limits from 35 to 40, income tests on four different magnitudes, caps from 300 € to 1,500 €, and frequent extra conditions (deposit of the bond, landlord NIF, minimum rent-to-income ratio, no other dwelling nearby).
4. **2026 changes found in the compendium** (L2): Cantabria (Ley 5/2026: rental extended to under-40 with two or more children; new young-buyer and housing-savings deductions from 1 May 2026), Cataluña (Ley 8/2025: rural rental, purchase and rehabilitation deductions), Andalucía (Decreto-ley 1/2026: storm-damage rental, purchase and repair), Castilla-La Mancha (Ley 1/2026 amendments and a new housing-savings deduction), Extremadura (Ley 2/2026 amendments), Comunitat Valenciana (Ley 3/2026 amendments to rental and disability purchase), Illes Balears (Ley 4/2026 rental and landlord amendments; temporary 2026 variable-rate mortgage deduction).
5. **No regional housing deduction affects payroll withholding.** The withholding procedure (arts. 80–89 RIRPF) has no input for regional deductions; its only housing input is the DT 18ª reduction in art. 86.1 (L1). Regional deductions apply only in the annual return.

## State rules

### S1. New rental deduction, art. 68.6 LIRPF (RDL 29/2026)

| Field           | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Who             | Tenant of their habitual dwelling.                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Rate            | 10 % of amounts paid in the tax period for the rent of the habitual dwelling.                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Income test     | BI **strictly below** 33,007.20 €.                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Maximum base    | BI ≤ 23,007.20 €: 11,630 €. Between 23,007.20 € and 33,007.20 €: 11,630 − 1.163 × (BI − 23,007.20).                                                                                                                                           |
| Other condition | For at least half of the tax period, neither the taxpayer nor any member of their family unit holds full ownership or a right of use over another dwelling less than 50 km away, unless an administrative or court decision prevents its use. |
| Quota           | Fully deducted from the **state** quota (art. 67.1.a, as amended).                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Compatibility   | Not compatible with the DT 15ª transitional rental deduction for the same dwelling and period (new DT 15ª.3). The text states no incompatibility with regional deductions.                                                                    |
| Withholding     | None. RDL 29/2026 amends RIRPF arts. 41 bis, 69, 75, 76, 93 and 94 only; the employment withholding articles are untouched.                                                                                                                   |
| Source          | LIRPF art. 68.6 and art. 67.1, added/amended by art. 6.Segundo.Tres–Cuatro RDL 29/2026, BOE núm. 249, 7 October 2026 (BOE-A-2026-20823). **L1.**                                                                                              |

**Open questions (Pending):**

- **Validation.** A Real Decreto-ley lapses unless Congress validates it within 30 days of its promulgation (art. 86.2 Constitution). The identical RDL 26/2026 was repealed on 2 October 2026 (BOE-A-2026-20526). Until RDL 29/2026 is validated, treat S1 as provisional.
- **Temporal scope.** The IRPF changes in that block apply "con efectos desde la entrada en vigor" (8 October 2026), while the deduction refers to amounts paid "en el período impositivo". The text does not say whether rent paid between 1 January and 7 October 2026 counts. No AEAT guidance was found. Do not assume either reading.
- **Family unit and joint filing.** The 50 km test refers to the family unit, but the text gives no joint-filing cap. Whether the 11,630 € base applies per return or per taxpayer is not stated.

### S4. Landlord: reduction of net rental income, art. 23.2 LIRPF (context only)

This is a reduction of the landlord's net income, not a quota deduction. It matters because several regional landlord deductions refer to it, and because the calculator only models rental income for foral profiles today.

- **Contracts after 1 December 2026 (L1, art. 23.2 as amended by RDL 29/2026):** reductions of 100 %, 95 %, 90 %, 85 %, 70 %, 50 %, 40 %, 30 %, 25 %, 20 % or 15 % depending on whether the landlord is a large holder, whether the new rent drops, stays or rises compared with the previous contract, the reference-price index, stressed-market zones and tenant age (18–35). Other cases: 70 % for public or non-profit tenants in social renting, 60 % after a recent rehabilitation, 80 % during tacit extensions within the index.
- **Contracts up to 1 December 2026 (L1, DT 38ª.2):** the art. 23.2 wording in force on 31 December 2025 applies. Contracts before Ley 12/2023 keep the wording of 31 December 2021 (DT 38ª.1). **Pending:** transcribe those two older wordings from their BOE versions before implementing.
- **Pending:** the exact boundary between "up to 1 December 2026" and the new regime, which is ambiguous in the text.

Withholding on rental income received by the landlord is not employment withholding and is out of scope.

## Annual return versus payroll withholding

| Rule                                               | Annual return        | Payroll withholding                                                   |
| -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| S1 new state rental deduction (68.6)               | Yes (state quota)    | No                                                                    |
| S2 transitional rental (DT 15ª)                    | Yes                  | No                                                                    |

Consequences for the calculator: housing deductions change the annual result and refund, never the monthly net pay, except the DT 18ª withholding reduction. Quota deductions cannot make a quota negative; several regions allow carrying unused amounts forward (AST-3, Galicia landlord deductions), which a one-year estimator cannot represent.

## Inputs the calculator would need

Grouped by the phase that first needs them.

- **Tenant, phase 2:** annual rent paid; rent subsidies received; contract start date (and whether pre-2015); whether the taxpayer is the contract holder; bond deposited (yes/no); landlord NIF available (yes/no); ITP-AJD filed (Murcia, La Rioja, Extremadura); other dwelling owned and its distance (50, 70 or 75 km tests); municipality population or its rural/depopulation classification; group flags (large or single-parent family, gender-violence victim, unemployed ≥ 183 days, widow(er)).
- **Income magnitudes:** savings base (the engine currently assumes employment income only in the common regime). Each region uses a different test: BI general + savings; BI total − Min. P&F; BL general and BL savings separately; BI before minima. These must be modelled explicitly, not approximated with the current `annualBase`.
- **Buyer, phase 3:** purchase date (pre-2013 for DT 18ª), price, amounts paid in the year split into principal, interest and costs; protected-housing status; new build; first dwelling; mortgage type and interest of the previous year (Balears, Valencia, Madrid); net-worth increase (patrimony test).
- **Family unit:** sum of bases of all members (Madrid 61,860 € and 30,930 € × members), joint vs individual filing.
- **Landlord, phase 4:** gross rent, expenses, contract dates, tenant age, stressed-zone status, reference-price index, number of dwellings owned, empty period.

## Implementation plan

Each phase is a separate PR with its own sources in `data/fiscal-sources.json`, independent fixtures and UI strings in all five languages.

1. **Phase 0 – data model and guards.** Add a housing section to the input model with explicit "not provided" defaults; add a per-rule provenance table (rule id, community, level, legal article, verification date). Results must state which housing rules were applied or skipped and why. No calculation changes.
2. **Phase 1 – state transitional rules (S2, S3).** Stable by construction. Includes the only payroll effect (DT 18ª −2 points), which needs the form 145 input. S1 waits behind a flag until RDL 29/2026 is validated and its temporal scope is clarified.
3. **Phase 2 – regional tenant deductions, general profiles.** For each community, implement the deduction for the common profile (age-based young tenant) first: MAD-1, CAT-1, VAL-1, EXT-1, CLM-1, CYL-1, GAL-1, BAL-1, AND-1, AST-1, MUR-1, RIO-1, CANT-1, CAN-1 (after resolving "renta"). Model each income magnitude separately. Add the savings-base input. Promote each rule from L2 to verified-in-regional-text before merging.
4. **Phase 3 – municipality-dependent rules.** Needs a dataset of municipality population and regional depopulation/rural lists (`js/data/cities.js` has no population). Covers rural rental and purchase variants.
5. **Phase 4 – buyer deductions.** Purchase-year amounts, interest split, patrimony test, multi-year rules. Many are long-tail; prioritise by community population.
6. **Phase 5 – landlord side.** Requires a common-regime rental income module (art. 23 reductions by contract date, DT 38ª) before any regional landlord deduction.
7. **Out of scope until joint filing exists:** joint-filing caps and family-unit income sums. Show the individual-filing result and say so.
