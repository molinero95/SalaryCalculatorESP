# Fiscal validation and supported assumptions

Reviewed on 9 October 2026. Tests use fixed official figures and independently worked examples. They do not download live tables in CI: a legislation change requires a reviewed data and fixture update. Published table quotas can differ from unrounded integration by up to a cent; tests allow that publication rounding. Annual tax and withheld amounts are returned in cents.

## Engine boundaries

`js/tax.js` owns pure income-tax rules. `js/calc.js` assembles contributions, flexible compensation, pensions and cash payments. `applyScale` remains re-exported by `calc.js` for existing callers.

Withholding and annual assessment have separate minima: the AEAT withholding algorithm explicitly uses age ≥65/≥75, while annual assessment follows the >65/>75 wording in LIRPF art.57. For official scales, annual state and regional quotas are individually nonnegative. For explicitly edited combined scales below the reference regional share, the hypothetical state adjustment may be negative so that the requested combined tax remains unchanged. A regional minimum exceeding the base does not reduce state tax. The €600 descendant reduction affects withholding only. Employer pension income is imputed for annual net income before applying pension deductions and the 30% limit; the common €1,500 room also applies to employment contributions.

## Profiles and limits

- Individual assessment, a single employment payer, a complete year and no other income, savings, carryovers or income irregularities. Family situation selects withholding thresholds, not joint assessment.
- Children and ascendants entered are assumed to satisfy the statutory income, cohabitation and filing conditions. Ascendants are fully counted; shared ascendant eligibility and disability of relatives are not represented. Child sharing is selectable.
- The temporary-contract option models the minimum withholding rule for a contract shorter than one year. Contracts lasting a year or longer need the general withholding procedure; contract duration is not a separate input.
- Minimum contribution base defaults to €1,424.40 (monthly groups 4–7). Other contribution groups, daily/hourly minima, professional accident tariffs, pension-plan contribution rebates and special regimes require different inputs/rules. Part-time scaling is an estimate, not an hourly contribution calculation.
- Health insurance assumes the employee is the only disabled insured person, and that the aggregate premium can use the modeled per-person limits. A per-person premium/disability breakdown is not collected. Meal days and employer childcare/training are assumed to meet legal exemption conditions.
- Foral profiles outside the expanded active-employee/individual-assessment scope described in [locations.md](locations.md) and [foral-family.md](foral-family.md), Ceuta/Melilla/La Palma reductions, pensioner/unemployment profiles, judicial annuities, maternity/family credits and regional deductions are not calculated. The project does not infer deductions from child counts or age without the required eligibility inputs.
- Annual results remain estimates. A passing test proves a covered rule or case, not a full Renta WEB declaration.

## Verification

```bash
npm test
npm run test:coverage
npm run lint
npm run test:e2e
```

`test:coverage` reports the executed fiscal modules; browser reports and full-page captures are retained by CI. Existing macOS pixel baselines are separate and have not been regenerated by this fiscal change.
