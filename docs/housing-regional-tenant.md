# Regional tenant rent deductions (housing phase 2)

Phase 2 of the [housing plan](https://github.com/molinero95/SalaryCalculatorESP/pull/22) calculates the regional deduction for rent of the habitual dwelling for the **general profiles** of each common-regime community. Code: `js/domain/housing-tenant.js`; guards and skip reasons: `js/domain/housing.js`; provenance: `js/data/housing-rules.js` and `data/fiscal-sources.json` (group `housing.regionalTenant`); fixtures: `tests/housing-tenant.test.js`.

Fiscal year 2026. Texts checked on 10 October 2026. Changes published after that date are not covered.

## What is calculated

The deduction is a claim against the **regional quota** of the annual return only. It is capped so the regional quota cannot become negative and it never changes payroll withholding (the withholding procedure has no input for regional deductions).

## Inputs and guards

- **Formal requirements** (`housingRegionalConfirmed`): a single confirmation covers the contract holder and habitual dwelling in the community, bond deposit or lease tax where required (Madrid, Galicia, Murcia, La Rioja, Extremadura), landlord NIF in the return (Andalucía, Cataluña, Castilla-La Mancha), payment without cash (Murcia, Valencia), lease duration and date conditions, and the "no other own dwelling nearby" tests (Valencia 50 km, Balears 70 km, Extremadura 75 km, Murcia > 50 %). Without it every regional tenant rule is skipped with `requirementsNotConfirmed`.
- **Savings base** (`housingSavingsBase`): the common-regime engine does not tax savings income. The value is used only in the income tests above and in the DT 15ª base test; the UI says so.
- **Family unit** (Madrid): art. 18 excludes the deduction when the bases of all family-unit members exceed 61,860 €. The person must confirm and enter the other members' bases (0 if none); otherwise MAD-1 is skipped with `familyIncomeNotProvided`. Individual filing alone does not satisfy that condition.
- **Groups** (`housingLargeFamily`, `housingSingleParent`, `housingTwoMinorChildren`) are explicit declarations because each community defines them by its own law. Groups that are **not collected**, so the deduction may be missed but never over-applied: victims of gender or domestic violence or terrorism, unemployed ≥ 183 days and widow(er)s over 65 (Cataluña), intellectual or mental disability ≥ 33 % (Valencia), separated parent with two children (Extremadura), dependants with disability and self-employed (Balears), over 65 not working (Balears: the calculator models employees).
- **Rent aid** (`housingRentAid`) is subtracted where the text deducts only unsubsidised rent (Murcia, La Rioja).
- **Minimum**: the personal and family minimum is the state amount of the annual return. Cataluña, Murcia and Castilla-La Mancha do not have their own minima, so state and regional amounts coincide for the rules that test it.

## Interpretations (flagged for review)

- **Asturias and Balears** test the "base imponible" without saying general or savings; the sum of both is used, as in the AEAT reading of similar regional limits.
- **Galicia**: "as contías" are doubled with a disability ≥ 33 %; the euro caps are doubled, not the percentage (AEAT Renta 2025 reading).
- **Valencia**: art. 4.Uno.n) (as amended by Ley 3/2026) refers to the limits of art. 4.Cuatro. The consolidated text of art. 4.Cuatro could not be read in full with the tools available (the page is truncated); the 27,000/30,000 € limits and the cap-only scaling come from the Ministry compendium _Tributación Autonómica. Medidas 2026_ (updated 23-09-2026) and the AEAT Renta 2025 manual, which scales the cap and gives a worked example. Re-check art. 4.Cuatro directly before treating VAL-1 as fully verified.
- **Castilla-La Mancha**: the BOE consolidated text was last updated on 31-01-2023 (450 € cap). Ley 1/2026 replaced apartado 1 of arts. 9 to 9 quinquies (500 € cap). Its entry-into-force provision could not be read in the DOCM extract, but the regional tax administration applied the same law's IRPF changes to 2025 (consulta 75/2026), so the 500 € cap governs 2026 either way. The descendant minimum is subtracted in the income test (art. 9.3.b), which the compendium omits. Arts. 9 ter to 9 quinquies prorate their cap by days of lease; the calculator assumes a lease in force all year.
- **Madrid**: the art. 8 and 18 wording comes from Ley 5/2024; later Madrid laws were not read, but the Ministry compendium of 23-09-2026 reports the same values for 2026.
